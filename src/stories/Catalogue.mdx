# Everything, on one page

Twenty-one primitives, and the app is built out of them. Each one is embedded from
its own story rather than rebuilt here, so this page cannot drift — pick one in
the sidebar under **Components** for its variants and the reasoning.

Everything below takes its colour, size, spacing and shape from
`src/styles/tokens.css`. Change a value there and every one of these moves, on
every screen, at once. That is the whole point, and
`tests/unit/design-system.test.ts` is what keeps it true.
